Monster Parts

HomeWikiChange LogClass CommandsGM ScreenTown builderTreasure plannerCharacter builderNPC builderItem builderEncounter builderMap prepMonster Parts

Run Salvage creature parts at any table: find a creature and see which skills can take parts from it, the DC of each, and the parts each skill gives on a success. The same rules as the bot’s 🦴 Salvage button — this page is written from the bot’s own rule. A Table Goblin optional rule, not a Paizo one: Paizo prints how parts are used (Monster Crafting, Craft Requirements that name a hide or a tooth) but no check for getting them.

  1. Search or filter the bestiary: name, level, trait, rarity, size, book.
  2. Pick the creature that died (on the enemy or neutral side — never an ally).
  3. One character rolls one check with a skill they are trained in (it takes 10 minutes): the skill decides the parts. One attempt per creature for the whole party.

The rule

  • Who: a creature that died in the fight on the enemy or neutral side — never a player character, companion, summon or ally, never a humanoid or giant.
  • The check: one per creature for the whole party, with a skill the character is trained in (or better). It takes 10 minutes.
  • The DC: GM Core’s DC for the creature’s level, +2 uncommon, +5 rare, +10 unique. A fitting Lore stands in for any skill at an easier DC: one step easier; a Lore about the creature itself (Dragon Lore, Manticore Lore) two.
  • Critical success: two different parts of the roller’s choice (two of it when the skill has only one). Success: one. Failure: nothing. Critical failure: the remains are ruined.
  • A part is named after the creature (Manticore Fang; the GM can rename it), is tagged monster part, has the creature’s level, no Price, Light Bulk, and can’t be sold. It is what a crafted item is “made from”.
