Item builder

Make homebrew weapons, armor, shields and equipment for your campaign, and see at once how each one compares with the printed items: die sizes, traits, Armor Class, Hardness, item bonuses and price for its level. The checks are advice, not rules. GM Core's tables are labeled as such (Starfinder 2e has no item-building chapter of its own, so its items use "GM Core (applies to SF2e)"), and every comparison worked out from the printed items is marked [derived]. Nothing here is uploaded; your work is kept in this browser until you download the file. Premium coming: when subscriptions start, /tgm homebrew add, edit and import will need a campaign subscription (free while we test).

  1. Add an item and pick its system and type. Start from a printed item, or from scratch.
  2. Set its stats. Damage, traits, AC or Hardness, an equipment item's bonuses, level and price.
  3. Read the checks on the right, along with the three closest printed items. Only a broken item or a printed item's name is refused; everything else is your call.
  4. Download the file and run /tgm homebrew import in your campaign with it attached.

A quick one? /tgm homebrew add makes a simple item in Discord, and this page can copy that command for you. Equipment (worn and held items) can give a bonus to Perception, a skill or Lore, saves, AC or attack rolls -- the kinds a printed item worn or held the same way gives -- and resistance to a damage type, which work while a character has it invested; spell activations are coming.
