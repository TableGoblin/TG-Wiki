Pocket trinkets

HomeWikiChange LogClass CommandsGM ScreenTown builderTreasure plannerPocket trinketsCharacter builderNPC builderItem builderEncounter builderMap prepToken creatorMonster Parts

Worthless little things a defeated foe might carry: a lucky pebble, a half-written letter, a child's carving. They make the people your party fights feel like people. None of them has a Price, Bulk or any game effect. Roll one here at the table, or turn on the Pocket trinkets campaign rule and Table Goblin adds them to the loot of defeated creatures for you.

  1. Choose the lists: untick Silly to leave out the crude or goofy trinkets, or Story hooks to leave out the ones that hint at a story you may not want to follow up.
  2. Pick the creature's ancestry, or General for any humanoid.
  3. Press Roll a trinket to get one. A creature with its own list draws from it half the time and from the general list otherwise.

The lists

In Discord: /tgm campaign_rules (or the GM dashboard's Rules button) has the Pocket trinkets rule, off until you turn it on, with the same two filters. A creature qualifies by its ancestry trait (skeletons, automatons, poppets, leshies, sprites, awakened animals and conrasu have lists too) or, with no ancestry, the humanoid trait. Mindless creatures and animals carry nothing. In the loot a trinket shows as 🧸 Trinket, so nobody mistakes it for treasure: it can't be sold and weighs nothing.
