HomeWikiMap prepCharacter builderChange Log

Plan a 1st-level character and its starting gear here, where searching for equipment is easy, then download a recipe file. In Discord, give it to /tgchar build as tg_file: the bot checks every choice against your campaign's rules, works out the sheet itself, and asks you for anything this page doesn't cover. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

1Your campaign's rules

Your GM can give you the code (it is pinned in the character channel). Or tick the rules by hand:

A campaign code fills this in. Without one, set it yourself (leave it blank for no limit); it travels in the file so the bot knows how far your campaign has got, and the bot enforces the party level when you import anyway.
