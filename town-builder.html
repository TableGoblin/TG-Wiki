Town builder

HomeWikiMap prepCharacter builderNPC builderTown builderTreasure plannerChange LogClass Commands

Roll up a settlement for your table: its name, its shops, the merchant behind each counter, and what is on the shelves. It uses the same tables and stocking rules as /tgtown generate: the town's level sets what can legally be sold, a bigger place has more and more specialist shops, and the shelves lean toward the best things the shop is allowed to carry.

  1. Set the level (and the size, or let the level choose it), then press Roll a town.
  2. Press 🎲 beside any name, ancestry, personality or note to roll just that again, or type your own.
  3. Give a shop a level bonus to let it stock above the town's level, then Restock.
  4. Copy as text or Print it. Your town is kept in this browser.
