NPC builder

HomeWikiMap prepCharacter builderNPC builderTown builderTreasure plannerChange LogClass Commands

Build one creature or a whole cast at the power level you mean, using GM Core's Building Creatures tables. Every number has Extreme, High, Moderate and Low buttons for the creature's level, and the page warns you when something runs past the table. Nothing here is uploaded; your work is kept in this browser until you download the file.

  1. Pick a level, then a tier for each number. Change the level and every tiered number follows.
  2. Add Strikes, spells and gear. A weapon fills its die, damage type and traits; the tier sets the attack and damage.
  3. Check the warnings on the right: numbers past the table, too many Extremes, gear above the safe item level.
  4. Download the file and run /tgnpc bulk_add in your campaign with it attached.

Rather use a spreadsheet, or build in Discord? Download the CSV template, or run /tgnpc build.

    Spreadsheet import (CSV)

    Download the CSV template

    One creature per row; save it as CSV (UTF-8) and attach it to /tgnpc bulk_add. Run the command with no file for the same template. Everything is checked first: one problem and nothing is added, and every problem is listed with its line. The template's first row is a whole custom NPC; the second is a bestiary creature.

    Tier words. Any stat, attack bonus, Strike damage or spell DC can be a number or a tier word (extreme, high, moderate, low, terrible), and GM Core's table fills in the number for the creature's level, the same numbers as the buttons above. An empty stat on a custom NPC is Moderate (Intelligence: Low).

    ColumnsWhat goes in them
    creatureAn exact bestiary name. Filled stat columns replace its own; Strikes, spells, gear and abilities are added to it. Leave empty for a custom NPC.
    name, alias, countA custom NPC needs only a name. The alias comes from the name if empty; count 3 makes shark1, shark2, shark3.
    adjustment, level, size, rarity, traits, significantelite or weak (bestiary rows); level −1 to 24; Tiny to Gargantuan; traits separated by ;; yes for PC-style dying.
    ac, hp, temp_hp, hardnessNumbers or tier words (HP takes the middle of the table's range; it has no Extreme, so it uses High).
    fortitude, reflex, will, perception, sensesModifiers or tier words; senses separated by ; (darkvision; scent (imprecise) 30 feet).
    speed, fly_speed, swim_speed, climb_speed, burrow_speedFeet.
    str, dex, con, int, wis, chaAttribute modifiers or tier words.
    acrobatics … thieveryOne column per skill (16); empty means none. Lores aren't kept on creatures.
    resistances, weaknesses, immunitiesfire 5; cold 5, silver 5, poison; sleep. An exception goes in brackets: physical 5 (except silver). Hits are matched by what they are as well as their damage type: a swarm has resistances physical 5 and weaknesses splash-damage 5; area-damage 5 (also critical-hits, persistent-damage, energy, cold-iron, silver).
    strike1_name … strike4_*Up to 4 Strikes. A real weapon's name (Longsword, Shortbow) fills its die, damage type, traits and range.
    strikeN_attack_bonus: +15 or a tier.
    strikeN_damage: 2d8+7 slashing, or a tier (high, or high piercing for a natural attack); a tier is built the GM Core way, with the extra damage counted toward the table's average.
    strikeN_additional_damage: 1d6 fire. strikeN_traits: agile; finesse. strikeN_range: feet, empty for melee. strikeN_reach: feet.
    spell_tradition, spell_type, spell_dc, spell_attackarcane / divine / occult / primal; prepared / spontaneous / innate; a number or extreme / high / moderate (the attack follows the DC's tier if empty).
    slots_rank1 … slots_rank10Slots per rank. Empty for a prepared or spontaneous caster: GM Core's slots for the level.
    spell1_name … spell12_*Up to 12 spells. spellN_rank: the rank it's cast at (empty: its own rank; a cantrip is always 0).
    spellN_uses: an innate spell's at will, constant or 2/day; a prepared spell's number of copies.
    gear1_name … gear6_*Up to 6 real items, with gearN_qty and gearN_runes (+1; striking; flaming). The creature carries them, and they drop as loot when it is defeated.
    ability1_name … ability6_*Text on the sheet: abilityN_actions (1, 2, 3, reaction, free, or empty for passive) and abilityN_text.
    sneak_attackDice, like 2d6. Applied in combat: a Strike at an off-guard or flanked target adds it, and the sheet offers it beside each Strike.
    rage_damage, rage_ac, rage_temp_hpThe creature's Rage (4, -1, 10). Applied in combat: the Combat tab's Rage action gives the temporary HP, then melee Strikes add the damage and AC takes the penalty until raging ends.
