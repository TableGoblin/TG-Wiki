Encounter builder

HomeWikiMap prepCharacter builderNPC builderTown builderTreasure plannerEncounter builderChange LogClass CommandsGM Screen

Plan a fight before the session: pick creatures from the bestiary (weak, normal or elite) and the custom creatures you made in the NPC builder, and see its XP budget rating for your party, the same rating /tge begin shows you in Discord.

  1. Choose the game system and set the party's level and size.
  2. Search the bestiary and add creatures as weak, normal or elite, or open your NPC builder file and add its creatures.
  3. Read the rating: the XP total against GM Core's Trivial to Extreme budgets for your party.
  4. Export the encounter file and start the fight in Discord with /tge start encounter_file: (add a map_slot for its map), or place the creatures on the Map prep page first. It is kept in this browser.

Rating

GM Core's encounter budget: each creature's XP by its level against the party's (−4 to +4; a creature further below counts as −4, one further above as +4), with weak one level lower and elite one level higher (two at the low end). The budgets are for four players; each extra or missing player moves them by 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 / 40.
