Token creator

Turn any picture into a round map token with a ring around it. Everything happens in your browser: your picture is never uploaded or stored anywhere, and the finished token is a PNG you download.

  1. Choose a picture, then drag it into place and zoom until the face or figure sits nicely in the circle.
  2. Pick a ring: painted, metal, carved runes, wood, stone and more, with its thickness and color.
  3. Remove the background if you like, and fill behind the figure with a color or gradient.
  4. Break the ring so a hat, a horn or a sword tip sits in front of it, then download the size you need.

Ring size

The ring color paints the Painted styles. Tick Tint every style to shade metal, wood, stone and the rest with it too. A smaller ring size leaves room for a pop-out to rise above the ring.

3Background

Remove the picture's background
Tolerance28
Shrink edge0 px

Remove background clears the color running in from the picture's edges, so it works best on a plain backdrop. Then use the Magic wand on any patch it missed, and Erase or Restore to touch up by hand. Removed parts show faintly while one of those tools is on.

Fill behind the figure

The fill shows wherever the picture is see-through, so remove the background first.

4Break the ring

Paint with Pop out over the parts that should sit in front of the ring; Push back undoes it. Only the picture shows there, so remove its background first.

Line

5Download

A PNG with a see-through outside. 512 px suits most maps. To use it in Table Goblin, upload it with /tgchar set_avatar for your character, or /tgnpc set_avatar for a GM's creature. The bot checks every uploaded image for inappropriate content, the same as any other avatar.
